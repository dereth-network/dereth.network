Linked characters

To link a character, type @dn link in game while you're on the Dereth Network, and open the link it gives you. Keep the link to yourself.

Friends

Tells on Discord

A @tell to one of your linked characters while you aren't playing comes to you as a direct message from the Dereth Network's bot and you can reply right there as that character. Squelches and blocks still apply.

Discord lets the bot message you only while you share a server with it, and let that server's members send you direct messages (in Discord, the server's Privacy Settings).

Forget me

Takes every linked character off the Dereth Network, unlinks every linked character, forgets your friends, and signs you out everywhere. Unless you're also a world operator, it forgets your Discord account too.

Each character will keep the on-world friends it had.
