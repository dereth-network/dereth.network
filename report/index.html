Report a world

If a world on the network pretends to be another, abuses players on other worlds, or spams them, tell the people who run the network here. They see who sent each report; the world you report never does.

About one character? Squelch them in game, /squelch An Adventurer@leafcull, or their whole world, /dn squelch leafcull, and tell your own world's staff, who can block a character for everyone there. If their world lets them go on, report the world and name them in the details.

Sign in to report

Reports need a Discord sign-in, so each one comes from someone. The hub keeps your Discord id and name, and nothing else about you.
